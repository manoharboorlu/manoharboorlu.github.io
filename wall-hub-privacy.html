Wall Hub Privacy Policy

Wall Hub is a personal, open-source wall dashboard. It runs entirely on the owner's own device (a Samsung Galaxy Tab S7+ running Termux). There is no Wall Hub server, no account system, and no developer-operated backend.

What it accesses, and why

With the owner's explicit consent through Google's OAuth screen, Wall Hub uses these Google scopes:

  • Google Calendar (read-only): to show the owner's upcoming events on the dashboard.
  • Google Tasks (read and write): to show the owner's tasks and to mark them complete or incomplete when tapped.
  • Google Drive (read-only): to download photos from a folder the owner chooses, for the photo slideshow.

It also sends the owner's configured latitude and longitude to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to fetch the weather forecast.

What happens to the data

  • Data is fetched directly from Google to the owner's device and is shown on that device only.
  • Calendar, task and photo data is cached in files on the device so the display still works when Wi-Fi is down. It is not copied anywhere else.
  • Access tokens are stored in a file on the device. They are never sent to the developer or any third party.
  • Wall Hub does not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke Wall Hub's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete all cached data by deleting the cache folder and token file on the device.
